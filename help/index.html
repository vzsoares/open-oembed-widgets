How to use

These are tiny widgets you can drop into Notion (and many other tools) — no account, no code. Here's the whole flow in four steps.

  1. 1

    Pick a widget

    On the home page, scroll the cards and find one you like — clock, weather, a countdown, a button list, and more.

    Clock
    07 : 04
  2. 2

    Make it yours

    Tap the option buttons to change how it looks and switch light/dark. The preview updates as you go.

    Style Flip Analog tap to change
  3. 3

    Copy it

    Hit Copy to grab the widget's link. That's the only thing you need to paste into your tool.

    …/clock/?theme=light&style=flip Copy
    click to copy
  4. 4

    Paste where you want it

    In Notion: type /embed, paste your link, and click Embed link. Most other tools accept the same link in their embed block.

    /embed → paste link → Embed link

    Your widget appears, with a transparent background that blends right in.

Good to know

  • Theme. Widgets are monochrome and follow light/dark automatically. Pin one with the theme button if your tool doesn't pass it through.
  • Editing later. Paste any widget link back into the box at the top of the home page to tweak it again.
  • Privacy. No account or keys — everything runs in your browser.
